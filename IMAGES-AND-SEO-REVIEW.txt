IMAGE PLACEHOLDER GUIDE
Homepage and detail pages use designed placeholders so no broken image links appear.
When approved client images are ready, place them in images/page-images/ using these filenames:
ipd.jpg, opd.jpg, daycare.jpg, naturopathy.jpg, hot-water-immersion.jpg, panchakarma.jpg, vamana.jpg, basti.jpg, nasya.jpg, raktmokshana.jpg, yoga.jpg, dip-diet.jpg, chronic-kidney-disease.jpg, diabetes.jpg, thyroid-conditions.jpg, chronic-liver-disease.jpg, cancer.jpg.
Each detail page shows its suggested filename. Replace its .image-placeholder div with an img element whose src is /images/page-images/FILENAME.jpg and whose alt text accurately describes the image. Replace homepage .seo-card-image and .disease-image-slot placeholders in the same way.
Use approved, non-graphic, accurate images; obtain consent for identifiable patients.

SEO / CLINICAL REVIEW
Pages have unique title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, internal links, and basic structured data. The homepage has Hospital structured data and canonical metadata. The sitemap lists the new pages.
The clinical text is deliberately cautious and informational. A qualified clinician must review and approve each disease/therapy page before publication. Do not promise cures or guaranteed outcomes, use unverified claims, or encourage replacing prescribed care.
SEO improvements cannot guarantee rankings. Useful original content, accurate contact details, page speed, Google Search Console monitoring, relevant local listings and ongoing updates still matter.
