What to know about Diabetes: Information and Care Conversations

Diabetes is a condition involving elevated blood glucose. Type, duration, other health conditions and individual circumstances influence monitoring and treatment. Ongoing care may include glucose assessment, medication review, nutrition, activity and checks for complications.

Topics to review with your clinician

  • Your blood glucose records and recommended tests
  • Medicines, doses, side effects and what to do if a dose is missed
  • Food, physical activity, sleep and practical lifestyle goals
  • Foot care, eye checks and other routine assessments

Questions to bring

  • What are my individual glucose targets?
  • How often should I check glucose and have follow-up tests?
  • What symptoms require urgent medical attention?
  • How should I coordinate dietary changes with my medication?
Important: This page provides general information, not a diagnosis or an individual treatment recommendation. Have the hospital's qualified clinical team review it before publication. Do not delay urgent or prescribed medical care based on website information.
