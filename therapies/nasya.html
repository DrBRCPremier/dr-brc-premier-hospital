What to know about Nasya: Consultation and Safety Information

Nasya is an Ayurvedic procedure involving administration of substances through the nose. Ingredients and technique may vary. Nasal products and procedures can cause irritation or other unwanted effects, so suitability should be assessed individually.

Questions to ask

  • Which substance is proposed and what are its ingredients?
  • What are the risks, contraindications and possible interactions?
  • Is it appropriate given your respiratory history, allergies, medicines or current symptoms?
  • What side effects should prompt you to stop and seek care?

Safety note

  • Do not place unverified oils or substances in the nose. Seek medical advice for breathing difficulty, significant bleeding or a severe reaction.
Important: This page provides general information, not a diagnosis or an individual treatment recommendation. Have the hospital's qualified clinical team review it before publication. Do not delay urgent or prescribed medical care based on website information.

How do I know what is appropriate for me?

Suitability depends on individual circumstances, medical history and clinical assessment. Contact a qualified healthcare professional for advice about your situation.

What should I bring to a consultation?

Bring relevant reports, prescriptions, a list of medicines and supplements, allergies, and questions you want to discuss.
