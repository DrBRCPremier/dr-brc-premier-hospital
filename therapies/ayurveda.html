Understanding Ayurveda

Ayurveda is a traditional system of health knowledge originating in India. It includes concepts relating to daily routines, diet, individual constitution and therapeutic practices. The term covers a wide range of approaches; the specific service offered should always be explained clearly before a patient decides whether to proceed.

What to expect from a consultation

A consultation should include a review of health history, current symptoms, existing diagnoses, medicines, supplements, allergies and relevant investigations. Ask the practitioner to explain the purpose of any proposed recommendation, the evidence and limitations, possible risks, alternatives and follow-up arrangements.

Questions to ask before starting

  • What are the practitioner's qualifications and relevant registration?
  • What exactly is proposed, and why is it appropriate for my circumstances?
  • What are the possible risks, contraindications and alternatives?
  • Could this interact with prescribed medicines or ongoing treatment?
  • How will the plan be reviewed, and what should I do if symptoms change?
