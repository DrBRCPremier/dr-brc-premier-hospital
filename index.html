Your questions, answered

Hospital FAQs.

Quick answers to common questions about services and appointments at Dr BRC Premier Hospital.

What services are available at Dr BRC Premier Hospital?

Our services include IPD (in-patient care), OPD consultations and Daycare services. Contact our team to confirm availability and whether a service is suitable for your needs.

Can I take an OPD consultation virtually?

Yes. Virtual OPD consultations are available, subject to appointment availability. Please contact the hospital team to arrange one.

How do I book a consultation?

Call our team at +91 98102 85906 or use the Book a consultation / WhatsApp options on this website.

Where is the hospital located?

Dr BRC Premier Hospital is located at 792-P, Sector 42, Gurugram, Haryana – 122002.

How can I learn whether a therapy is appropriate for me?

Discuss your health history and questions with a qualified healthcare professional. Suitability and care decisions depend on an individual assessment.

Can I enquire about admission or Daycare?

Yes. Please contact our team to ask about admission enquiries, Daycare scheduling and service availability.
